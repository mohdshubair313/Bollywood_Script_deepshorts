# 🎬 BollyScript — Video Script (4-5 Minutes)

**Audience:** Classmates / Assignment evaluator  
**Tone:** Simple, confident, slightly dramatic (it's Bollywood!)  
**Visuals:** Screen recording + slides

---

## [0:00 – 0:30] Intro — What is BollyScript?

**(On screen: App landing page — gold title, dark theme, tagline visible)**

**You (on camera or voiceover):**

> "What if I told you — you type *'Two friends fighting over a coffee cup'*… and within seconds, you get a full Bollywood movie script? Title, cast, dialogues, stage cues, background music — the whole package."
>
> "That's BollyScript. An AI-powered web app that turns any boring real-life situation into a masala Bollywood screenplay."
>
> "Built with Next.js, Tailwind CSS, and AI agents running on Groq's lightning-fast LLMs."

**(Cut to screen recording)**

---

## [0:30 – 1:15] How to Use It — Super Simple

**(Screen recording: typing in the text box)**

> "Using BollyScript is embarrassingly simple. Look at this."

**(Click into input field, type something)**

> "You see this text area? Just describe any situation. Keep it short — three words is enough. *'Late for wedding.'* *'Cat steals food.'* *'Boss yells at me.'*"

**(Click "Generate Script" button)**

> "Hit this gold **Generate Script** button… and watch the magic happen."

---

## [1:15 – 2:15] What's Happening Inside? — The 3 AI Agents

**(On screen: Split view — left side shows UI loading bar, right side shows agent diagram)**

> "Now, here's the cool part. Behind the scenes, BollyScript doesn't ask one AI to do everything. It uses **three specialized AI agents**, each with a specific job."

**(Animate each agent appearing one by one as you speak)**

> "**Agent 1 — The Title Architect.** Its job? To invent a movie title, tagline, genre, and mood. So your *'Late for wedding'* becomes *'Dil Ki Dawat'* — *The Feast of the Heart*."

> "**Agent 2 — The Casting Director.** It creates 3 to 5 characters. The hero, the villain, the comic sidekick. Each with a name, a role, a description… and a signature quirky catchphrase."

> "**Agent 3 — The Screenplay Writer.** The star of the show. It takes the title from Agent 1 and the cast from Agent 2, and weaves them into 2 to 4 full scenes. Each scene has dialogues, stage directions, and even background audio cues."

**(Pause for effect)**

> "Three agents. One pipeline. One complete movie script."

---

## [2:15 – 3:00] How the Pipeline Works — Agent 1 → Agent 2 → Agent 3

**(On screen: Flow diagram — Input → Agent 1 → Context → Agent 2 → Context → Agent 3 → Output)**

> "Let me show you how they talk to each other."

**(Highlight Agent 1 box)**

> "Step one: Your situation goes to Agent 1. It generates the title and concept."

**(Arrow animates from Agent 1 to Agent 2)**

> "That concept gets injected into Agent 2's prompt. So the Casting Director knows it's a romantic comedy, not an action film, and creates characters accordingly."

**(Arrow animates from Agent 2 to Agent 3)**

> "Then both the concept AND the cast get injected into Agent 3's prompt. The Screenplay Writer knows exactly who the characters are and what kind of movie it is."

**(Final arrow to Output)**

> "The result? A script where every scene matches the title, every dialogue fits the character, and everything feels… consistent. Like one person wrote it."

> "This is called **prompt chaining** — the output of one agent becomes context for the next."

---

## [3:00 – 3:30] The Loading UI — See the Progress

**(Screen recording: showing the loading-state component with 3 progress bars)**

> "Now, all this takes about 7 seconds. But instead of staring at a spinner, you see THIS."

**(Point to each agent row as it progresses)**

> "A loading bar with three stages. **Title Architect** — waiting… working… done. **Casting Director** — same thing. **Screenplay Writer** — and then we're live."

> "Each row has a status icon — waiting, spinning, or a green checkmark. The active agent glows with a golden border."

> "You always know *which* agent is working and *what* it's doing. No black box. No mystery."

---

## [3:30 – 4:30] The Results — Cards, Scenes, Dialogues

**(Screen recording: scroll through the generated output slowly)**

> "And when the script arrives? It's beautiful."

**(Pause on MoviePosterCard)**

> "First, a movie poster card. The title in gradient gold… the tagline… genre and mood badges. It instantly feels like a real film."

**(Scroll to CastCard)**

> "Next, the cast. Each character in its own glass card — name, role badge, description, and their quirky catchphrase at the bottom. Hover over one and it glows."

**(Scroll to SceneCard)**

> "Then the scenes. Each scene has a number, a title, a setting description…"

**(Click on stage cue badge)**

> "See that **stage cue** badge? It pulses with a golden glow and has a clapperboard icon. Pure Bollywood drama."

**(Click into a dialogue)**

> "And the dialogues. They appear as chat bubbles — alternating left and right, like a real conversation. Character name in gold or purple. The acting direction in italic. And the line in bold white."

**(Point to audio badge)**

> "Every dialogue has a background audio cue too — *[Violin crescendo]*, *[Dramatic drum roll]* — shown as a cyan glowing badge with a music note icon."

**(Pause)**

> "Even if something goes wrong, there's a Bollywood-themed error card with a retry button. Because in Bollywood, every setback is just a dramatic intermission."

---

## [4:30 – 5:00] Closing — Why It Matters

**(Back to face cam or full screen of the app)**

> "So here's why BollyScript is more than just a toy."

> "It demonstrates **prompt chaining** — how multiple AI agents can collaborate on a complex task."

> "It shows **structured outputs** — how to make AI return valid JSON that you can render directly."

> "And it proves that **developer experience matters** — the loading bars, the glassmorphism cards, the glowing badges. Good UI makes good tech feel amazing."

**(Smile, hold up coffee cup or do a dramatic gesture)**

> "BollyScript. Because every situation deserves a dramatic close-up."

**(Screen fades to black with gold text: "BollyScript — Made with AI, Written with Drama")**
